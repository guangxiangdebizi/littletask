# Product

## Register

product

## Platform

ios

## Users

LittleTask is for people who coordinate real work and relationships inside chat: founders, operators, consultants, recruiters, sales professionals, and anyone who receives meeting commitments or contact updates in screenshots. They need to move from conversation to action without manually retyping details or trusting opaque automation.

## Product Purpose

LittleTask turns a chat screenshot and optional note into grounded, editable Action Cards. It creates or updates contacts and calendar events only after the user confirms the exact payload. After execution, it combines the approved action with relevant contact, calendar, and prior interaction context to suggest useful next steps.

Success means users can review the source evidence, correct ambiguity, and complete a real action faster than manual entry without losing control of their data.

## Positioning

LittleTask is the confirmation layer between an informal conversation and a real-world system change.

## Brand Personality

Observant, controlled, and concise. The product should feel like a careful operations partner: it notices details, separates fact from assumption, and never acts behind the user's back.

## Anti-references

- A generic purple AI chatbot with an empty prompt box.
- A dashboard made from interchangeable rounded cards and fake metrics.
- A task manager that hides the source conversation after extraction.
- Automation interfaces that celebrate speed while obscuring what will be changed.
- Dense CRM screens that make a one-minute personal workflow feel administrative.

## Design Principles

- Keep source evidence adjacent to every proposed action.
- Make uncertainty more visible than confidence scores.
- Use progressive disclosure: summary first, exact fields and diffs when reviewing.
- Let the user confirm one stable revision, then make execution status unambiguous.
- Treat privacy and local execution as product behavior, not legal footer copy.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Use native touch behavior, VoiceOver labels, Dynamic Type-compatible hierarchy, 44-point minimum touch targets, sufficient contrast, non-color status labels, reduced-motion support, meaningful loading/error/empty states, and responsive layouts down to narrow mobile screens. Web acceptance surfaces target WCAG 2.1 AA.
